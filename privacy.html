Blood Sugar Ninja

Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

Your health observations stay out of analytics. Only belt progress is backed up to Firebase, after sign-in and explicit consent. Glucose readings, guesses, trends, symptoms, target ranges and sensor credentials are not uploaded to Firebase. Optional usage analytics are off until you enable them. There are no ads or advertising trackers.

Data stored on your device

The app stores completed guesses, the corresponding glucose reading and trend, symptom tags, dates, target ranges, belt progress, and settings in a local database. Insights and the guess journal are calculated on your device. Demo history is kept separate from personal history. Without sign-in, game history remains on the device. If you sign in and enable backup, only belt progress is backed up to your account in Firebase. Full guess history stays on this phone. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple backup settings.

Optional accounts and belt backup

This describes the current belt-only backup. Earlier test builds could upload full game history after sign-in. Those legacy cloud records were deleted on October 9, 2026; only belt award IDs and points were preserved. The current app does not read or add to those legacy collections.

Google or Apple sign-in uses Firebase Authentication to store your account identifier, email address and any name supplied by the provider. Cloud Firestore stores belt progress in the United States. It does not receive glucose readings, guess values, trends, symptom tags or target ranges. Demo records stay on the phone and are not uploaded. Security rules limit each account to its own belt progress. Firebase processes network information needed to provide these services; see Firebase privacy information.

Signing in does not automatically upload history. Before enabling backup, you choose whether to move saved belt awards from this phone into the signed-in account or keep those awards on the phone. Signing out stops backup and returns to guest belt progress. All real glucose and guess history on this phone remains visible regardless of the signed-in account; demo history stays separate. Delete account in Settings removes the account and its cloud and local belt progress, while retaining the phone’s glucose and guess journal. If deletion is interrupted, Settings shows a retry state. Other devices may retain a local copy until removed. A deletion marker without glucose or game history is retained to prevent other installations from uploading the deleted history again. Cloud belt progress otherwise remains until you delete the account. Sensor passwords are kept in Keychain and are never sent to Firebase.

Connecting Dexcom

If you connect, your username and password are kept in the iOS Keychain. Your username is also stored locally with connection settings. Credentials and connection requests go directly to Dexcom Share over HTTPS; they are not sent to us or PostHog. Dexcom processes these requests under its own privacy policy. The app receives your readings from Dexcom and stores the readings associated with saved guesses locally.

The app uses an unofficial Dexcom Share integration, not the published Dexcom OAuth developer API. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Dexcom. This connection may change or become unavailable.

Optional usage analytics

If you enable “Share usage analytics” in onboarding or Settings, selected usage and reliability events go to PostHog in its US region. Events include app launches, screens visited, rounds started and completed, connection attempts and outcomes, operation durations, safe error categories, and names of settings changed. Setting values are excluded.

Events include a random installation identifier, session identifier, timestamp, app version, and build number. The persistent installation identifier connects activity from the same installation; these events are not described as anonymous. We use them to understand usage and improve reliability. We do not send your name, Dexcom identity, glucose readings, target values, guesses, symptom tags, health outcomes, passwords, or raw server responses.

Session replay, automatic event and screen capture, and automatic crash reporting are disabled. IP-based location enrichment is disabled and IP event properties are removed; PostHog necessarily receives the network connection. We do not use analytics for advertising, cross-app tracking, or selling data.

Your choices, retention, and deletion

Analytics are optional. Turning them off in Settings stops new analytics collection; it does not automatically delete events already received. Previously sent events remain in PostHog subject to the project's retention settings until deleted. For questions or a deletion request, contact us below. We may be unable to associate an installation's events with your email because account identity is not linked to the analytics installation identifier.

Saved guesses remain on the device until the app's data is deleted. Deleting the app removes its local database; offloading the app does not. Manage any device backups through Apple's settings. Disconnect Dexcom in Settings to delete the saved Keychain credentials; deleting an iOS app alone may leave Keychain entries. Disconnecting does not erase saved guess history.

Reminders and safety settings

Practice reminders are local notifications, optional, and scheduled only with your permission. They do not send glucose data to a push service. They cannot check your glucose when delivered and are not low-glucose alarms. An observed very-low reading pauses reminders until a fresh non-very-low reading is received. The very-low pause preference and the date you acknowledge disabling it are stored locally, not in analytics.

Children and families

A parent or guardian should supervise use by children. Analytics are disabled by default and are not required to play. Optional account sign-in can provide a name and email address. A parent or guardian should manage account setup for children. Contact us with privacy concerns about a child's use.
