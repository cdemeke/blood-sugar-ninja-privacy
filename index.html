How it works

Three small steps, one quiet superpower.

  1. Pause and feel

    Take a moment to notice how you feel. Shaky? Hungry? Totally fine? Tag what you notice. Never delay a needed glucose check.

  2. Make your guess

    Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard mode and make your guess. Use demo mode to explore with sample readings.

  3. Reveal, together

    The number counts up and the ninja reacts kindly. The reading itself is never good or bad — it's information, and noticing it is the skill.

What's inside

Built gentle, on purpose.

A playful moment to notice how you feel, compare a guess, and reflect on your saved observations.

Grows with your ninja

Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard mode, from broad categories to a numeric guess. Follow your progress in the game at your own pace.

Feelings come first

Tag how you feel: shaky, sweaty, tired, hungry, or fine. Look back at your saved observations and explore possible patterns.

Plays nice with Dexcom

Connect your own compatible Dexcom Share account. Launching in the US. Readings may be delayed or unavailable. Demo mode uses sample readings so you can explore before connecting.

Progress, kindly counted

Review your guessing accuracy and saved history. Scores describe your guesses, not your health or your ability to make treatment decisions.

Why this exists

Inspired by being a parent.

Inspired by my experience as a parent, I created Blood Sugar Ninja to make noticing how you feel more playful. Make a guess, compare it with your CGM reading, and explore your patterns over time.
